# Direct academic prose revision — v0.2.8

This revision applies Kiterlin's anti-defensive-writing skill to the eight
prose sections of the manuscript. The skill is pinned to commit
f516147d017a3fe43ae99192cb89d8261b0c8679 in
https://github.com/Kiterlin/anti-defensive-writing.

Repeated anticipatory rebuttals are replaced with direct descriptions of the
study, its measurements and their scope. Limitations that determine how a result
can be interpreted remain with the relevant method or result and in Discussion.
The abstract and conclusion retain workflow-derived labels and the July
logistic reversal. The introduction and related work now state the contribution
and its relationship to earlier methods more directly.

## Examples

| Earlier wording | Revised wording |
| --- | --- |
| Retrieval settings do not determine a policy-independent candidate set. | Realized candidate sets depend on the commitment policy as well as the retrieval settings. |
| Neither a net recall decrease nor an interval containing zero establishes that later evidence never helps. | A net recall decrease or an interval containing zero is compatible with later evidence helping individual records. |
| The trigger audit further limits an explanation based on evidence from new arrivals. | The trigger audit separates claims at new arrivals from claims at other deadlines. |

## Scientific content and verification

All numerical tokens, mathematical expressions, citation keys, section headings,
definitions, proofs, algorithms, figures, tables and captions are preserved.
The official template and bibliography are unchanged. The figures and their
standalone plotting archive are identical to v0.2.7.

The manuscript retains the fixed-stream uncertainty scope, exploratory May and
retrospective July design, selected and uncertain workflow labels, coupled
retrieval/waiting effects, single-fit scorer reversal, recovered and lost
positive pairs, conditional graph bounds, historical external-linker scope,
Louisville annotation and resampling limits, and instrumented runtime scope.
